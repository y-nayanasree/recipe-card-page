Creamy Herb Pasta

A creamy, comforting pasta recipe with garlic, herbs and cheese. Perfect for a quick and delicious meal!

⏱️10 minPrep time
🍳20 minCook time
🍽️2 servingsServes

💎 Ingredients

  • 200 g pasta
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • ½ cup cooking cream
  • ¼ cup grated cheese
  • 1 tsp mixed herbs
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley for garnish

💎 Preparation Steps

  1. Boil pasta in salted water according to the packet instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add cream, mixed herbs, salt and black pepper. Stir gently.
  4. Add cooked pasta and toss until coated with the creamy sauce.
  5. Mix in grated cheese and cook for one more minute.
  6. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve warm.
